The concept is a single meeting participant who, with one ordinary sentence near the end, accidentally reveals that the whole meeting never needed to happen. The joke lands as a small scene rather than a general complaint about meetings: a named person, a trivial fact, and a room full of laptops closing at once.

> The meeting ended when Dave said, 'The client approved this last Tuesday,' and nine laptops closed at once.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This validates the one-line entry (one line, 1–200 characters, no control characters) and writes it to `entry.txt` in `$OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), with no trailing newline.
